About Us

CA. Amit Arora Classes is an institute designed for aspiring professionals, providing the knowledge and skills essential for building a successful career. Our faculty, with advanced degrees and expertise in specialized fields, have come together with a shared mission to redefine education.

At AAC, we are dedicated to guiding students through every stage of their journey toward success. Our focus is on building a solid foundation of knowledge and skills that will serve as a springboard for students to ascend the ladder of professional excellence.

AAC was founded in 2016 by CA. Amit Arora, inspired by his passion for delivering exceptional educational services to students pursuing CA, CMA, and CS qualifications. In a remarkably short period, AAC has grown significantly, attracting a large student base due to its high standards of education and consistently excellent results establishing itself as one of Best institutes in Delhi, India.

AAC offers flexible learning options to cater to the diverse needs of students, we provide:

  • Face-to-face classes for an interactive, in-person experience,
  • Direct-to-Home (D2H) live classes that allow students to learn from the comfort of their homes in real time.
  • Recorded classes are available for those who prefer to study at their own pace, providing the flexibility to access lessons anytime, anywhere.
